# ReqAI — UX Principles

## Product identity

ReqAI is a requirements management system with embedded AI capabilities. It must not look like a generic AI chat application.

## Primary principles

1. Workflow before AI
2. Data and traceability before decoration
3. AI appears at the point of decision or assistance
4. Dense information should remain scannable
5. Every AI result must be reviewable and editable
6. Human approval remains explicit
7. Status, ownership, history and auditability are first-class concepts

## Visual direction

- Professional B2B SaaS
- Neutral surfaces
- Restrained accent color
- Strong typography hierarchy
- Tables and forms for management workflows
- Tabs for requirement detail
- Breadcrumbs for context
- Badges for lifecycle status
- Minimal decorative gradients
- Minimal animation
- Accessible contrast and keyboard navigation

## Requirement workspace

The workspace is the flagship experience. It should feel like an enterprise specification editor with contextual assistance, not a chatbot.

Suggested information architecture:

```text
Project / Requirement
────────────────────────────────────────
Header: ID · title · status · owner · actions

Tabs:
Overview | Specification | Acceptance Criteria | Rules | Quality | Questions | History

Main content: structured requirement information
Right/contextual panel: analysis and assistance
```
